# Final information and article timing

The two-stage lifecycle keeps a saved initial judgment and schedules its final review at T-20. Initial reports prefer an off-peak slot. A final review follows the game deadline even when it falls in a peak tariff period.

## Current information

The scheduler starts warming information during the last hour, with checks at T-60, T-35, T-25 and the existing final-window slots. This does not claim a generation task or publish an early final. Collection refreshes game identity, current team membership, official injury information and market reference. Team membership is not a game active list.

When structured lineup fields are empty, bounded English and Chinese searches look for game-specific reporting. DeepSeek Search/Fetch is a server-side acquisition option; body acquisition also retains direct-HTTP and existing extraction fallbacks. Search snippets are discovery only. Fetched claims must pass source, quote, player/team identity and time validation. Source projections never become confirmed starters. Explicit game active lists can support eligibility; absence from an injury report cannot.

The optional `final_information_policy: latest_verified` permits a final analysis with labeled expected lineups and unresolved availability. It still requires current membership, no confirmed-Out starter conflict, qualified market data, injury-source provenance and legal pregame timing. A current partial injury report is disclosed as partial. Unknown availability does not become Available, and cannot support a full-strength assertion. The prior strict `confirmed_only` behavior remains available; legacy policy identities do not change without explicit configuration.

Accepted claims and source times survive restarts. Searches have a five-minute cooldown; the collector reuses game-bound evidence without inventing a new publication time. The incremental assessment reuses the saved basketball baseline and updates only affected reasoning. Basketball assessment remains isolated from market input. The final version is append-only and retains baseline/delta/readiness evidence.

## Articles

Pregame prose leads with a short concrete story and spends most of its space explaining the side, spread and total judgments. Names and numbers serve the analysis. Postgame prose uses the locked pregame version and saved result, separates winner/spread/total outcomes, and explains which assumptions held or failed without inventing play-by-play.

New titles contain two seven-character Chinese lines. A single title-only correction can use two arrays of seven individual characters. A valid same-game baseline title may be retained for a final with unchanged material basketball assumptions, subject to the existing factual check. Existing articles and version payloads are not rewritten.

## Postgame timing and delivery

Result records are deterministic and can be saved immediately after final facts arrive. The optional Writer defers during configured peak hours, without consuming an attempt. At the dated DeepSeek tariff used here, Beijing weekdays 09:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00 are peak; the other hours are off-peak. Holiday exceptions use the configured calendar. Pricing is not a wallet reconciliation.

An accepted Postgame article has its own publication job tied to the pregame version, Postgame payload and article hash. A reviewed article revision has a new delivery identity, while the frozen record and original article remain intact. The same pregame ID does not make a new Postgame delivery a duplicate. Delivery uses the existing isolated-checkout queue; network retries never generate another article. A failed Writer preserves the deterministic record and the last accepted public page.

The program supplies the signed team spread labels and selected side/total outcomes. New articles receive finite checks for those labels, explicit final scores, named player points, unsupported player-period descriptions, unobserved sequence claims and length. A failed draft has at most one article correction and one title correction, within three Writer calls per attempt. Publication rechecks the selected article. These checks do not constitute a general basketball entailment or causal-analysis evaluator.

The backend and publication queue remain local. The computer, local reader, network and credentials must remain available. Passing offline tests or an isolated real-model rehearsal does not prove an upcoming scheduled generation has succeeded.
